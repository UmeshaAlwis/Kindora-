Kindora is a technology-driven social enterprise platform designed to connect donors, charities, and businesses within a transparent and engaging digital ecosystem. The platform enables charities to raise funds, sell products, and showcase real-time impact, while allowing donors to contribute through secure, personalised, and recurring donation options. By integrating AI-powered recommendations, impact tracking, gamification, and a transparency dashboard, Kindora strengthens trust, improves donor engagement, and supports sustainable charitable funding. Kindora operates on a sustainable revenue model through small transaction service fees, marketplace commissions, and corporate CSR partnerships, ensuring long-term platform growth while maintaining ethical transparency.
